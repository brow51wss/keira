01 — Colors

Onyx, gold and beige

Keep the balance near 60% beige/ivory, 30% onyx, 10% gold. Alternate light and dark sections down the page; never place two dark sections back to back without a light one between.

Onyx
Primary #0B0A09 · --hk-onyx Header, hero, footer, dark sections, dark buttons. Hover: #221F1B.
Gold
Secondary #D4AF37 · --hk-gold Primary buttons, script accents, lines and text on onyx. Hover: #E6C458.
Beige
Tertiary #F5EBD7 · --hk-beige Page background and light sections. Pair with Ivory #FBF6EC for cards and alternate sections.

02 — Font families

Three fonts, three jobs

Aa Playfair Display Headings · H1–H5, quotes Regular 400 for H1–H2, Medium 500 for H3–H5, Italic 400 for pull quotes. Fallback: Georgia, serif.
Aa Montserrat Body · H6, labels, buttons, forms Regular 400 for body, Medium 500 for eyebrows and labels, SemiBold 600 for buttons. Fallback: Helvetica Neue, Arial.
Aa Great Vibes Accent · script Short phrases only ("My Stage.", "Keira"). One per section, never for body copy, never smaller than 28px.
